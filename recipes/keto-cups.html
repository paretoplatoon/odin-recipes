Steak Recipe

This is a delicious steak recipe that you can try at home.

Description

Juicy, tender, and full of flavor, ribeye steak is the crown jewel of beef cuts. With its rich marbling, it cooks to perfection whether seared on a cast-iron skillet or grilled over open flames. A simple seasoning brings out its natural flavors, making it a showstopper for any dinner.

Instructions

Ingredients

  • 1 ribeye steak (1 to 1.5 inches thick)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 2 garlic cloves, smashed
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • Fresh rosemary or thyme sprig

Steps

  1. Pat steak dry and season with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat skillet on high, add olive oil.
  3. Sear steak 2–3 minutes per side.
  4. Add butter, garlic, and herbs; baste steak.
  5. Rest 5 minutes before slicing.
